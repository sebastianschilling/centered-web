Support

Write to centered@sebastianschilling.com – questions, bug reports and feature ideas all end up with the person who wrote the app. Answers usually take a day or two.

If something went wrong with a specific video, it helps to know which iPhone and which iOS version you are on, how long the clip is, and what the app said on screen.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • Portrait orientation; the app is built for the phone, not for iPad
  • Access to your photo library, to load the video you pick and to save the result

Questions that come up

I bought the watermark removal and got a new phone. How do I get it back?

Open “About Centered” from the start screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. The purchase also travels through Family Sharing.

The app says “No person detected”.

The detection needs a person that is actually visible: reasonably lit, not hidden behind equipment, and large enough in the picture. If your clip is a hard case, you do not have to give up – switch to Correct, use Add person and drag a frame around them on a few positions along the timeline. Your points override the automatic tracking, and the camera path is built from them.

One person was detected as two.

That is what Combine is for: tap the frames that show the same person, then combine them into one.

The tracking looks wrong and I want to start over.

Tap Analyze → Analyze video. That throws away the stored result for this clip and detects everything again from scratch.

How do I get rid of the watermark?

One in-app purchase, €4.99, no subscription. You’ll find it in the export view and under “About Centered”. Every video you export afterwards is free of it.

Loading the video takes forever.

If the clip only lives in iCloud, iOS has to download it first – the app shows the progress and lets you cancel. Analysis itself runs on the device and depends on the length of the clip and on your iPhone model.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple as well: reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell me what went wrong anyway – if it is a bug, I want to fix it.

What happens to my videos?

They stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and no usage data is collected. The details are in the privacy policy.
