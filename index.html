How it works

Three steps. The expensive part – finding the people – happens once; after that the preview reacts to every slider in milliseconds.

Screenshot · VideoStart screen with the illustration and “Select video”.
Step 1

Video

Pick a clip from your photo library. Trim the start and the end on the timeline. Videos you worked on before come back under “Recent”.

Screenshot · TrackingColoured frames around two people, tool strip at the bottom.
Step 2

Tracking

Centered detects the people in the clip. Tap the ones you want to follow. Where the automatic detection was wrong, correct it: drag a frame, combine two frames that show the same person, or add a person by hand.

Screenshot · FramingVertical preview, format badge, zoom and ease sliders.
Step 3

Framing & export

Choose the format, set the zoom, decide how quick or how calm the camera should move – then save the result to Photos or share it.

What you can steer

More than one person

Follow a duo or a group – the frame holds all of them. Two detections of the same person can be combined into one.

Fixed, auto or manual zoom

Keep one distance, let the zoom follow the size of the person – wider when they are close, tighter when they are far away – or set it yourself with points along the timeline.

Now ↔ Ahead

Let the camera lead the movement instead of chasing it, by up to five seconds. It stays just as calm; it simply arrives earlier.

Quick ↔ Calm

One slider for the character of the move: snappy and reactive, or slow and unhurried.

9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9

Export for reels, for the feed, for a square post – or keep it wide.

Hand-held, not robotic

The camera move carries a deliberate hint of hand-held motion. It looks like someone filmed you, not like a crop that slides across a still frame.

Everything stays on your device

Your videos stay on your device. Centered analyses and renders everything locally – nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and no usage data is collected. Access to your photo library is used only to load the video you pick and to save the finished one.

That is not a promise about a server somewhere. The app has no networking code at all. Read the privacy policy.
